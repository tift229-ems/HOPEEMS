GA DPH & NREMT Clinical Standards Guide

Reference rules for student ride-alongs conducted through Hope EMS Training Academy in partnership with Berrien County EMS, Worth County EMS, and station clinical sites.

Student Shift Responsibilities

  • Submit completed field ride logs within 24 hours of shift conclusion.
  • Directly confirm all patient encounters with the assigned field preceptor prior to e-signature.
  • Maintain full HIPAA compliance; never record patient names, SSNs, or exact street addresses in chief complaint fields.
  • Verify accurate PCR numbers generated via GEMSIS Elite / ImageTrend systems.

Preceptor License Attestation Rules

  • Field preceptors must hold a valid Georgia Paramedic or AEMT license.
  • The preceptor must directly supervise all psychomotor skills performed by the student.
  • Checking the attestation box and entering an e-signature acts as an official legal verification for state accreditation audits.
